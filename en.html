    COMPANY PROFILE

    Texpro Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of Sainty International Group, specialized in hometextile design; development and production.

    Aiming at medium-high end home textiles for worldwide markets, with understanding of different market needs; knowledge of textile technology, passion of continuous creation, we are able to provide our customers with solutions to all their needs. With the support from the group, we are able to run big program for major customers smoothly and become key vendor for them. Our products of soft home (bed room, living room, bath room, kitchen/dinning room) kids & baby; pets and hospitality are mainly made in our own invested various factories thus we can fully control.
