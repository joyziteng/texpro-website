    COMPANY PROFILE

    Texpro Co., Ltd., originating from Sainty Group, is an independent home textile manufacturer with its own factories, specialized in a full range of home textile design, development and production.

    Serving home textile markets worldwide, we understand the needs of different regions and keep up with the latest textile technologies and innovations. This enables us to provide customers with solutions to all their needs. With the support of Sainty Group, we run large programs for major customers smoothly and have become a key vendor for them. Our products — soft home (bedroom, living room, bathroom, kitchen/dining), kids & baby, pets and hospitality — are mainly made in our own invested factories, so we can fully control the quality.
